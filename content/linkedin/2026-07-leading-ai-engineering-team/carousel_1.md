## Slide 1 - cover
AI is delivering real value now.
Good engineering practice still decides whether teams thrive — or burn out.

## Slide 2 - the problem
Engineers are overwhelmed.
Not because AI fails —
because the pace of change outruns shared context and judgment.

## Slide 3 - architecture
Solid architecture sets the frame and boundaries
AI should operate inside.
Without it, agents invent a new system every Monday.

## Slide 4 - RFCs and ADRs
RFCs debate the hard call.
ADRs keep the why.
Together they give humans and agents the context that prompts alone cannot hold.

## Slide 5 - 1:1s
1:1s build the relationship.
Trust, growth, hard news —
the part no agent can replace.

## Slide 6 - what changed on my team
One shared brain, not private models of the system.
More help, more sparring, better under pressure.
I trust their judgment more — and I can delegate.

## Slide 7 - closing
Keep the human judgment.
Write the decisions down.
Own the architecture together.
Agents amplify whatever frame you give them.
